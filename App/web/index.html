      Three simple steps

      You usually do not need to change any settings.

      1. Find the company. Search its name or certificate number. Choose the plant if needed.
      2. Check the preview. Details come from Excel. Choose the certificate type on this page; Excel details stay locked for first generation.
      3. Create PDF. The app chooses the COD, saves the PDF and updates the correct record.

      Company not in Excel? Choose New certificate. Saved certificates opens certificates already in your folder. After generating, Edit certificate opens corrections directly. Choose whether to update Excel when saving. After sending a PDF yourself, mark it Completed.

      Connection lost

      Your edits and saved files are kept. The app is not responding.

      Check the connection, or reopen Prepare Certificates. Before repeating a creation, check Saved certificates: the PDF may already have been saved.

      Create several certificates

      • Creates all Ready Excel rows in the selected plant, not just search results.
      • Empty statuses and N/A rows are skipped.
      • You will review any warnings before creation.
      Which filters apply?

      Only the plant selection applies. Search, status and certificate-type filters do not narrow this batch. Ready rows with warnings are included, but require your confirmation.
